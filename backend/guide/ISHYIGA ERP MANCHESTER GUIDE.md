# ISHYIGA ERP MANCHESTER GUIDE

Official how-to from Ishyiga Software Guide YouTube videos. Use these steps for Ishyiga Manchester and Ishyiga ERP. Always send the matching YouTube link with the steps. Do not add menus or buttons that are not written here.

## Make an order in Ishyiga Manchester and transform it into an invoice

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GMuTfTH02kg
Title: HOW TO MAKE ORDER AND TRANSFORM IT INTO INVOICE Using ISHYIGA MANCHESTER
Channel: Ishyiga Software Guide

This is the process for Ishyiga Manchester (also written Manschesta) and then Ishyiga ERP.

### Step 1 — make the order in Ishyiga Manchester

1. Open Ishyiga Manchester
2. Enter your credentials
3. Choose your menu (the video also selects tariff GROSSISTE on ALL TARIFF)
4. Select items
5. Put the quantity needed, then click ADD (window V_QUANTITY TAKING)
6. Click DELIVERY
7. Choose table, then Yes
8. Click SAVEDELIVERY, then Yes (the delivery window shows client, TIN, payment type, cash, reference number, and INEZA / VISA / V_MASTER)
9. Print or save as PDF (the video saves the file as ORDER)

### Step 2 — transform the delivery into an invoice in Ishyiga ERP

1. Open Ishyiga ERP (main gate tiles: people, ITEMS, SALES, PURCHASES, RRA, ACCOUNTING, TAX, E-COMMERCE, ANALYTICS)
2. Choose employee
3. Open the sales screen with setting DELIVERY-INVOICE, then click DELIVERY-INVOICE
4. Click OK for CASH or CREDIT
5. Choose payment type (the video uses CASH, print A4, then SAVE DELIVERY-INVOICE)
6. Print or save as PDF (the video saves the file as DELIVERY TO INVOICE)

The delivery-invoice window is titled TOTAL DELIVERY-INVOICE FACTURE CASH. After SAVE DELIVERY-INVOICE, confirm Yes, then print or save the PDF.

## Ishyiga ERP Software overview

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H3E8_87hOow
Title: ISHYIGA ERP Software
Channel: Ishyiga Software Guide

Ishyiga ERP is a business management system for sales, stock, invoicing, and reporting.

Processes shown in that video:

1. Open Ishyiga ERP MAIN GATE. Click Purchases to open the stock interface. Enter ID and PWD.
2. Use the Sales Management Interface.
3. New item creation: fill the form data, then click ADD.
4. Supplier: fill supplier information, then click ADD.
5. Stock / purchase: enter purchase price, search VIEW EVENTS and press it, add lines in the item window with ADD / REMOVE / COST, then OK.
6. SELECT ALL PRODUCT, then OK. Enter reference number and invoice number.
7. Statistics Report: Top Sales, Top Client, Top Employee.
8. Sales OPTIONS: Current or All, then Click Ok.
9. Create a client: fill the form, then click SAVE.
10. Generating Proforma on the Sales Interface: select the warehouse you are using, select client, enter the needed quantity, fill the proforma, then SAVE PROFORMA.
11. Invoice: choose payment type, SAVE INVOICE, then confirm by selecting Yes.
12. Select the option and press Enter. Open Z DAILY REPORT for sales and NS grossiste.
