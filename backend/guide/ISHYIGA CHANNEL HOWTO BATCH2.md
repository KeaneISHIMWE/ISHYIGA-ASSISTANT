## Umusada Guide - Ishyiga Software

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8PfqLUtEOmU

Umusada helps businesses in emerging markets access cash quickly. With invoice financing, companies get loans without physical collateral, using business data.

1. Open Your Browser (Chrome)
2. Search "Umusada.com"
3. Open join-us (Welcome — Join us to access your business dashboard)
4. Fill Email Address, First Name, Last Name, Business TIN, Phone Number, Password (minimum 8 characters). Rwandan TIN is 9 digits long (numbers only). Phone: enter the phone number registered with your business
5. N.B: TIN must be match with Phone number (RDB Certificate)
6. Login with same Email and Password (Welcome Back — Sign in to access your dashboard). Sign in, or Sign in with Google
7. Open Business Profile. Click on "Add Attachment" to Upload your Business Document. Attachment Type (the video shows RDD Certificate), Media Type Document (PDF, Word, Excel), Choose File, Upload. Requirements: PDF, Word, or Excel files only, max 5MB
8. Click on "Add Director" to Add your Directors. Personal Information: First Name, Last Name, Middle Name, Gender, Email Address, Phone Number, Document Type, Document Number, Document Attachments / Add Document, then Add Director
9. Click on "Add Account" to Add your Bank Account. Financial Institution, Account Name, Account Number, Business ID, then Add Account
10. Open Loans. Request New Loan: Your Business Score Limit, Financial Institution, Loan Type (General Loan or Invoice Financing), Loan Amount (RWF) (the video shows Minimum: RWF 50,000), Purpose, Request Loan

Do not copy example names, emails, or passwords from the video. Use the client's own details.
