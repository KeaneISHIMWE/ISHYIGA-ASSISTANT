User data deletion

This page explains how to delete data collected by the Ishyiga Software WhatsApp assistant.

  1. Email ishyiga.rwanda@algorithm.rw from a mailbox we can match to your account, or send a WhatsApp message to +250 798 687 932 asking us to delete your chat data.
  2. Include the WhatsApp number used in the conversation.
  3. We will delete stored messages, conversation history, and related customer lookup cache for that number, then confirm by email or WhatsApp.

See the privacy policy for what we collect and why.
